# VLC

[VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/) is free on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and Android. It plays kia's playlist, but it has no TV guide, so it's best for a quick look or for checking whether a channel plays.

## On a computer

1. Open VLC and choose **Media → Open Network Stream** (on a Mac, **File → Open Network**).
2. Enter the playlist address: `http://<your address>:8780/curated.m3u`, or a [profile's](profiles.md) playlist such as `http://<your address>:8780/kids.m3u`.
3. Choose **Play**. VLC starts the first channel. Open the playlist (**View → Playlist**) to see every channel and pick one.

The full list has well over a thousand channels, so a profile makes VLC's playlist much easier to use.
